Tarotio

A daily reading for your sign

One reading a day, dealt for your sign.

Three hands of five cards, face down. Answer the reader’s question to earn a glimpse, then keep one card from each hand. The three you keep become your fortune for today.

  1. Answer. Right first time earns two glimpses. Right on the second try earns one.
  2. Glimpse. Each glimpse turns one face-down card face up.
  3. Keep. Choose any card, seen or unseen. Skill opens your eyes. The deck still decides what was dealt.

Choose your sign

Your sign sets your deck, your own Major Arcana card, and the suit that shines brighter for you.

What you have seen

    Tarotio is a game for reflection and fun. The cards follow traditional Rider-Waite-Smith meanings. They are a mirror for your day, and your choices stay your own.

    How to play

    Each day one deck is shuffled for every star sign. It deals you three hands of five cards, face down. You keep one card from each hand: your Past, Present and Future.

    1. Answer the reader’s question. Right first time earns two glimpses. Right on the second try earns one. The questions grow harder from Past to Future.
    2. Glimpse. Spend each glimpse to turn one card face up and see how brightly it shines today.
    3. Keep one card. Keep a card you have seen, or gamble on one you have not.

    The moons

    Every card has a brightness for the day, from New Moon to Full Moon. It comes from the card’s traditional meaning and whether it lands upright or reversed. A reversed card is often dimmer, but the hardest cards turn kinder when reversed.

    No card is bad. New-moon cards are the ones that ask something of you.

    Skill and luck

    Your answers earn glimpses, and glimpses let you choose with your eyes open. But the deck is shuffled before you sit down and most of each hand stays face down. A perfect player can still be dealt a deep day. A blind draw can still turn up The Sun.

    Your reading shows both parts: Insight is what you earned, Fortune is what the deck gave. It also shows every reading the deck could have dealt, so you can see how lucky you were.

    Patterns

    The three cards are read together, the way a reader would. Cards in your sign’s suit shine one step brighter. Your sign’s own Major Arcana card, three of one suit, a repeated number, a run of numbers or three Major Arcana all add to your fortune.
